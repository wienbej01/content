### ENG-0505 — Hero Lipsync Continuity & Sync QA

**Owner:** Engineer

**Files:**
```text
scripts/media_service.py
tests/unit/test_hero_lipsync_qa.py
```

**Implementation steps:**
For `lipsync_video` or `generated_video` containing hero shots:
1. Compare final artifact duration against intended audio slice duration.
2. Fail if the duration delta exceeds an acceptable bound (e.g., > 100ms).
3. Record exact video and audio durations in QA evidence.
4. (Optional but recommended) Run a lightweight scene-cut detector (e.g., `scenedetect` or basic frame difference check) to flag unintended internal cuts in continuous hero shots.

**Required tests:**
1. Video shorter or longer than audio slice by >100ms fails QA.
2. Lipsync video with an internal hard cut fails (if detector implemented) or records a major warning.
3. Perfect duration match passes.
