## 1. Role in the Channel

James Harrington is the channel's sole host, teacher, and intellectual anchor. He is the reason the viewer is here.

He is not a brand mascot. He is not a narrator for b-roll. He is the person who has done the thinking and is now sharing the output. His presence in a video is a signal to the viewer that this content is personal, considered, and accountable.

In practical terms:
- **A-roll** (talking-head or present-voiceover): James is the visual subject; his presence is the delivery mechanism.
- **B-roll** (supporting visual): James may appear in the space (at a desk, walking, etc.) without addressing camera; b-roll can also show his world without him in frame. But the channel is not narrated over anonymous footage; James's identity is always implied.

## 2. Personality

| Trait | What it looks like in generation |
|---|---|
| Senior | Unhurried delivery, considered responses, no performance of effort |
| Thoughtful | Pauses before answering; looks at notes; will say "the question here is..." |
| Calm | No excitement jumps; no sudden vocal energy shifts; steady register |
| Competent | Does not hedge unnecessarily; speaks from experience, not theory alone |
| Understated | Dry, controlled humour; no self-congratulation; says less than he knows |
| Quietly intense | There is weight behind what he says; he cares about precision |
| Observant | Notices things others overlook; this comes through in what he notices visually |
| Intellectually generous | Explains the full model, not a simplified shortcut; gives the viewer credit |
| Credible | References come from experience, not Google; concrete, not abstract |

**James must not be:** fake-charismatic, influencer-hyperactive, motivational-speaker slick, over-rehearsed, artificially warm, or performatively humble.

---

## 3. Visual Identity

### Age impression
~60 years old. Not visibly unhealthy; not attempting to appear younger. The age is an asset — it implies the experience is real.

### Physical presence
- Build: moderate, controlled posture. Not muscular. Not slouched.
- Height impression: medium to tall.
- Movement: deliberate. Does not fidget. When he moves, there is a reason.
- Face: sharp features, weathered but not gaunt. Expressive eyes; controlled mouth.

### Baseline expression
Composed and attentive. Not smiling, not frowning — the expression of someone who is genuinely thinking. A very slight furrow of concentration is natural. A flat or vacant expression is not correct.

### Facial expression range

| Expression | Permitted use |
|---|---|
| Composed/thinking | Default; appropriate at all times |
| Slight engaged nod | When affirming a point; very subtle |
| Half-smile (earned) | Only when a dry observation lands or an idea clicks |
| Momentary pause with eyes briefly off camera | Collecting a thought; natural and permitted |
| Leaning slightly forward | When making a serious or important point |
| Holding expression through a pause | Stillness used as authority; permitted and encouraged |

**Forbidden expressions:** exaggerated surprise, open-mouth exclamation, motivational-grin, wide-eyed theatrical enthusiasm, pointing aggressively at camera, eyebrow-raise click-bait look.

---

## 4. Eye Contact Rules

- **Talking head (A-roll, direct address):** sustained, natural eye contact toward camera. Not staring; occasional breaks to glance at notes or papers are natural and correct.
- **Voiceover (James present, not addressing camera):** eyes on work, notebook, out the window, or thoughtfully downward. Eye contact with camera is not required and should not be forced.
- **B-roll (James present incidentally):** no expectation of eye contact; this is observational footage of his world.

---

## 5. Movement Rules

- All movement is purposeful and slow. James does not dart across frame.
- When walking (voiceover or b-roll), the pace is unhurried — a professional thinking through a problem.
- Gestures are controlled. If a hand moves, it is making a point, not filling space.
- James does not pace nervously.
- James does not lean against walls or lounge in chairs informally.
- James stands straight or sits upright; not stiff, but composed.

---

## 6. Behavior Rules

- James does not perform enthusiasm. When something matters, the weight comes through in stillness and precision, not energy.
- James does not perform modesty. He will make a strong claim when he believes it; he does not pre-apologize.
- James does not use filler gestures (finger guns, OK sign, index-finger points at camera).
- James does not check his phone on camera.
- James does not look directly into a laptop screen in a way that becomes the focus of a shot.
- When James is present in a space without speaking, he is doing something purposeful: reading, writing, reviewing a document, looking out a window.

---

## 7. Signature Gestures (Optional Recurring Motifs)

These gestures are not mandatory per video. They are recurring options that reinforce character consistency when used. Use sparingly — overuse turns them into tics.

| Gesture | When to use |
|---|---|
| Slight pause (1–2 seconds) before a key idea | Just before delivering the core insight of a section |
| Controlled hand movement — one hand, deliberate arc | When mapping out a framework or structure; not both hands waving |
| Brief glance at notes, then return to camera | Natural recall; signals that he is being precise, not improvising |
| Pen held lightly in one hand while speaking | Presence object; grounds his hands naturally |
| Closing a notebook or placing a pen down | Signals the end of a section or a concluded point |
| Slight forward lean, elbows toward desk | Making a serious or high-stakes point |
| Slow walk through the studio or a corridor | During voiceover; James thinking through the problem |
| Half-smile | Only when an observation lands; should feel earned |
| Extended stillness | Authority; holding the frame after a key statement |

---

## 8. Speaking Presence

- Voice: Received Pronunciation (Oxbridge RP). Locked to ElevenLabs settings (see `brand/BRAND_SPEC.md` §2). Do not re-generate or alter.
- Pace: calm, measured. The baseline from `brand/BRAND_SPEC.md`: 2.2–2.6 words per second for premium content; up to 3.0 for a teaser.
- Sentence rhythm: short declarative sentences, punctuated by occasional longer explanatory ones. Never rambling.
- Register: formal-casual. Fireside dinner with a senior partner, not a lecture hall.

---

## 9. Thinking Presence (Not Speaking)

When James is visible but not the direct subject of a shot — writing, reading, reviewing — he should appear absorbed and purposeful. This is not idle. He is doing real work.

**Correct:** James at a desk, reading a document, pen in hand, making an annotation. Unhurried.
**Incorrect:** James staring blankly into space. James looking up at camera with a smile when he isn't speaking.

## 10. James in Different Shot Types

### A-roll — direct address (talking head)
- Front-facing or slight angle toward camera
- Medium shot (chest-up) or medium close-up (shoulders-up)
- Studio/library setting (STUDIO_LIBRARY angles)
- Composed expression, occasional gesture, natural eye contact breaks
- Lipsync only if generated from the final approved ElevenLabs narration file for that episode

### A-roll — present voiceover (James visible, not addressing camera)
- James at his desk, or moving slowly through the space
- No camera address; absorbed in work
- May be at any approved studio angle
- Visual behavior reinforces the content being narrated

### B-roll — James incidentally present
- Establishing or transitional; James in the background or at the edge of frame
- Brief; not the primary subject
- Consistent wardrobe and environment

### B-roll — James absent
- Permitted; the world exists without James in every frame
- Must remain within allowed environments and motifs
- The universe's tone and quality must still be present

---

## 11. How James Must Never Appear

- As a different person in a different wardrobe, hairstyle, or age
- In a cyberpunk/futuristic/neon setting
- With an expression that reads as excited, shocked, or motivationally intense
- Performing gestures from the "forbidden expressions" list
- In generic stock-photo poses (arms crossed, forced smile, pointing)
- Without consistent reference — AI models must not invent a new James per session

## 12. Wardrobe

### Core wardrobe palette
Navy blue, deep charcoal, mid-grey, cream/ivory, dark forest green. Low saturation. No bright colors. (Consistent with `brand/BRAND_SPEC.md` §3.)

### Canonical outfit (default reference)
- Navy cashmere or fine-wool sweater
- Open-collar white or cream Oxford shirt visible at the neck
- Dark trousers (charcoal or navy; not visible unless standing shot)

### Acceptable outfits (variations)
| Outfit | Notes |
|---|---|
| Navy/charcoal blazer over open Oxford | More formal; appropriate for topic-driven arguments |
| Mid-grey fine-knit sweater over white shirt | Lighter; appropriate for a warmer-feel episode |
| Dark forest-green wool over cream shirt | Seasonal variation; same register as navy |

### Forbidden outfits
- Anything bright, patterned, or conspicuously branded
- T-shirts or casual hoodies
- Ties (too formal; changes the register from relaxed authority to performance)
- Suits (too corporate; wrong register)
- Loud colours (red, yellow, orange, electric blue)
- Sports or leisure wear

### Grooming
- Silver-grey hair, neatly combed back. Clean-cut, not coiffed.
- Clean-shaven. No beard variation between videos.
- No visible jewellery other than a plain watch (optional; if present, understated and dark-faced).
- No glasses (default; `OPEN QUESTION: if glasses are introduced, they must remain consistent`).

### Hand behavior
- Hands rest naturally on the desk or in the lap when not gesturing
- When gesturing, one hand at a time, controlled arc or flat plane movement
- Pen in hand is a natural resting state
- No fidgeting, no tapping, no phone handling on camera

## 14. Character Failure Conditions

| Failure | Category |
|---|---|
| James looks significantly younger, older, or differently built | Identity |
| James is wearing a forbidden outfit | Wardrobe |
| James beard or glasses introduced inconsistently | Continuity |
| James expresses shock, excitement, or influencer energy | Expression |
| James performs a forbidden gesture | Gesture |
| James is in a forbidden environment | Environment |
| James generated without reference assets, producing a different person | Generation |
| Lipsync generated without the final approved narration audio | Audio |
