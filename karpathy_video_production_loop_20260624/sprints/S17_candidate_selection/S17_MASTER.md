# Sprint 17 — Candidate generation and asset selection

## Objective

Generate multiple candidates for b-roll/non-lipsync assets, score them, and select the best with evidence.

## Sprint success criteria

- Candidate sets exist.
- Each candidate scored.
- Best candidate selected with reason.
- Rejected candidates logged.
- All-fail candidate group blocks assembly.
