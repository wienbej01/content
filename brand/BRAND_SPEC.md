## 2. The Persona — James Harrington

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Full name | James Harrington |
| Age | ~60 |
| Education | PPE, Oxford (late 1980s) |
| Career arc | Management consulting (late '80s–early '90s) → merchant banking, City of London → founded and exited two startups → venture capital (partner-level) → now: frameworks & systems for the next generation |
| Accent | Received Pronunciation (Oxbridge RP) — calm, measured, unhurried |
| Personality | Precise, understated, dry wit, evidence-led, generous with knowledge but allergic to hype |
| Aesthetic | Old money: tailored but not flashy, study/library settings, warm lighting |
| Real vs. generated | Unspoken. James simply *is* the presenter. |
| ElevenLabs voice | "James Harrington" — speed 1.05, stability 50%, similarity 75%, exaggeration 12% (locked — use for every content unit) |

### Voice & tone guide

- **Do:** speak with quiet authority; use concrete examples from "experience"; be direct; use occasional dry humour
- **Don't:** hype; use superlatives; say "game-changer" or "crushing it"; oversell; speak fast
- **Register:** formal-casual (a senior partner at a fireside dinner, not a lecture hall)
- **Sentence rhythm:** short declarative sentences, punctuated by one longer explanatory sentence. Never rambling.

## 3. Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary dark | Deep navy | #1B2A4A | 27, 42, 74 | Backgrounds, headers, thumbnails |
| Primary warm | Aged gold | #C8973E | 200, 151, 62 | Accents, highlights, CTAs, icons |
| Background | Warm ivory | #F5F0E8 | 245, 240, 232 | Page/slide backgrounds, light mode |
| Text | Charcoal | #2D2D2D | 45, 45, 45 | Body text |
| Accent | Oxblood | #6B1D2A | 107, 29, 42 | Subtle emphasis, secondary accent |

### Usage rules

- Navy + gold is the primary pairing (80% of surfaces).
- Oxblood is used sparingly — underlines, key data points, warning/important callouts.
- Never use pure black (#000) or pure white (#FFF) — always charcoal and ivory.
- Minimum contrast ratio: 4.5:1 for text on background.

## 4. Typography

| Role | Font | Fallback | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headlines / titles | Playfair Display | Georgia, serif | Bold (700) |
| Subtitles / labels | Playfair Display | Georgia, serif | Regular (400) |
| Body text | Inter | -apple-system, sans-serif | Regular (400) / Medium (500) |
| Captions / data | Inter | -apple-system, sans-serif | Medium (500) |
| Code / technical | JetBrains Mono | monospace | Regular (400) |

### Hierarchy

- H1: Playfair Display Bold, 36–48px
- H2: Playfair Display Bold, 24–32px
- H3: Inter Medium, 18–22px
- Body: Inter Regular, 16px, line-height 1.6
- Caption: Inter Medium, 12–14px, uppercase tracking +0.5px

### Rules

- Face always on the left (consistency = brand recognition)
- Max 6 words in headline
- Navy background variant OR ivory background variant (alternate)
- No "shocked face" expressions — composed, engaged, perhaps a slight knowing look
- Gold underline or frame on key word

## 6. Video Visual Style

### Setting

- Warm study/library: dark wood shelves, leather chair, warm lamp light
- Alternatively: clean desk with a single book/notebook and warm side-lighting
- Generated backgrounds (Higgsfield / Midjourney) — keep consistent across all videos

### Motion & pacing

- Unhurried cuts — hold shots longer than typical YouTube
- Kinetic text: Playfair Display for key frameworks, gold accent on critical words
- Diagrams: clean, minimal, navy lines on ivory, gold highlights
- Transitions: simple fades or hard cuts — no flashy swoops
- Lower third: "James Harrington | Leverage Mind" in Inter Medium, navy/gold

### Structure (per video)

1. Hook (5–10s): counterintuitive claim or relatable professional pain
2. Promise (5s): "Here's the system / framework"
3. Framework (3–5 parts): the original model, diagrammed
4. Worked example: "In my first year at [consulting firm]..." / screen demo
5. Takeaway + CTA: one clear action + product/newsletter link

## 11. Do / Don't Quick Reference

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Warm, muted, premium tones | Bright/saturated/neon colors |
| Serif headlines, clean sans body | All-caps sans-serif shouting |
| Library/study/desk settings | Offices, generic stock, outdoors |
| Composed expressions | Shocked face, exaggerated gestures |
| Unhurried pacing | Fast cuts, swoosh transitions |
| "In my experience..." framing | "YOU NEED TO..." aggressive CTAs |
| Dry wit | Internet humor / memes |
| Gold + navy accents | Rainbow or trending palettes |
